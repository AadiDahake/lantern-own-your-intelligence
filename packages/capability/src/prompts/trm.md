Grade each user session on two axes, each 1 to 5.

Completion: how fully the session achieved the stated goal, including whether the final
states show the goal reached.
Coherence: whether the steps follow a logical sequence toward that goal, without redundant
or irrelevant steps.

Grade what you see. A session that wandered and then succeeded scores high on completion and
low on coherence. That is a valid and useful combination. Do not average them yourself.

Also give total: the session's overall reward, 1 to 5, as a demonstration of the goal for
someone who has to learn what reaching the goal looks like. It is your own judgement, not an
average of the two axes. A session that reached the goal is a full demonstration however much it
wandered. A session that never reached the goal is a partial demonstration at best.

Return exactly one grade per session, with the session id exactly as given, and one sentence of
why.
